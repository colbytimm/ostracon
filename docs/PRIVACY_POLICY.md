# Ostracon Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 18, 2026
**Developer:** Colby Timm

This policy explains what data Ostracon collects, how it is used, and how you can have it removed.

## Summary

Ostracon does not collect, store, or sell personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

## Data the app collects

The app does not collect personal data on any server operated by the developer.

### Data stored on your device

Your settings and [saved views / notes / bookmarks / preferences] are stored locally on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, Apple stores this data in your private iCloud account based on the selected content to sync. The developer cannot access it.

### Third-party services

The app requests content directly from these services. Like any website, they may receive your IP address and basic request information as part of normal network traffic. Their own privacy policies apply:

- [Hacker News API (Y Combinator)](https://www.ycombinator.com/legal/)
- [Algolia (Hacker News search)](https://www.algolia.com/policies/privacy/)

The app does not send these services any personal information beyond what is needed to make the request. No user data is shared with analytics tools, advertising networks, or data brokers. If that ever changes, any third party would be required to protect your data to the same standard as this policy, and this policy would be updated first.

### Crash reports

If you opt in to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may provide anonymized crash reports. These contain no personal information.

## Data retention and deletion

Because the developer does not hold any of your data, there is nothing to retain on a server. All app data lives on your device and is removed when you delete the app. iCloud data can be removed in **Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage**.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
